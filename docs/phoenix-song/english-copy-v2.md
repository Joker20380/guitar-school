## A2 — Directions introduction

**Kicker:** 01 · LEARNING DIRECTIONS

**Heading:** Choose your path in music

Phoenix Song offers two directions: learning Russian through music and guitar, and exploring guitar and musical creativity. Choose the one that speaks to you!

## A3 — Russian through music

**Heading:** Russian through music

**Subheading:** Feel the Russian language from within

Would you like to do more than learn Russian words — to understand songs, feel their mood and get to know Russian culture through music?

We'll explore lyrics, work on pronunciation, play Russian songs on the guitar and discover the stories behind them. From Viktor Tsoi and Vladimir Vysotsky to the atmosphere of the films Brother and Brother 2… Let's discover this musical world together!

This direction is for people who are learning Russian and want to get to know the language through music, emotion and living culture.

## A4 — Guitar and musical creativity

**Heading:** Guitar and musical creativity

**Subheading:** Play what you love

Do you dream of playing the guitar, performing your favourite songs or trying to write one of your own?

I have a classical music education specialising in guitar, and I combine that foundation with modern playing techniques, accompaniment and performing popular songs.

I want you to develop good technique and an understanding of music, while also being able to play what you really enjoy, experiment with sound and find your own style.

And if you'd like to go further, we can try writing your very own song.

I believe in taking learning seriously — but not necessarily with a serious face.

## A5 — Why Phoenix Song?

**Kicker:** 02 · WHY PHOENIX SONG?

**Heading:** Why Phoenix Song?

When life turns you to ashes, music helps you rise again.

There have been times in my life when it felt as though almost nothing was left of who I used to be. When life was turning me to ashes, music helped me rise again — finding the strength to keep living, putting myself back together and always discovering something new within myself.

That's why the phoenix is more than just a beautiful symbol to me.

It's a reminder that even after the most devastating experiences, you can rise again and one day let your voice ring out even brighter than before.

That's why I created Phoenix Song. I want music here to be more than a skill you learn: I want it to be a source of inspiration, freedom and new discoveries.

## A6 — International community

**Kicker:** 03 · OUR INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY
